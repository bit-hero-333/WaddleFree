# WaddleFree

> ⚠️ **Disclosure:** This project was developed with heavy AI assistance ~~and is mainly public so I can benefit from GitHub's free Actions runners for automated APK and binary builds.~~ No warranty, no tech support, use at your own risk.

Ever felt too lazy to stand up from your bed to turn on your PC because you happen to be in that very niche group of people who use their gaming PC as a media server, but don't want it running 24/7? Well, it's your lucky day!

You just install both apps on your PC and mobile device to wake up your PC and put it back to sleep directly from your phone.

### Features
- **Local Connection**: Connects your PC directly to your mobile devices locally (via local IP and MAC address).
- **Quick Settings Tile**: Support for Android's notification shade tile to wake/sleep your PC with one tap.
- **Unsigned Executable Notice**: The Windows EXE is not digitally signed, so your antivirus or Windows SmartScreen will probably show a warning before running it.
